The SVAMITVA Vision

Ministry of Panchayati Raj, Govt. of India

SVAMITVA (Survey of Villages Abadi and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas) is a Central Sector Scheme providing a clear record of ownership to rural estate owners.

DroneMap AI accelerates this mission. It detects building footprints from map imagery and georeferenced drone orthomosaics, supports manual boundary review, and records the source evidence and measurements. Ownership and ground accuracy require independent verification.
