The SVAMITVA Vision

Ministry of Panchayati Raj, Govt. of India

SVAMITVA (Survey of Villages Abadi and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas) is a Central Sector Scheme providing a clear record of ownership to rural estate owners.

DroneMap AI accelerates this mission. By replacing manual ground surveys with high-precision edge AI and manual GIS tools, we instantly draw boundaries from drone orthomosaics or satellite imagery and generate provisional property titles (Form 7) for rural citizens directly into the registry.
