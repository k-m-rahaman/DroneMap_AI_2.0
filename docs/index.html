ILLUSTRATION

One connected survey workflow

AI suggests boundaries. A surveyor checks the result and keeps a traceable record of the changes.

01 / MAP

Choose your imagery

Select a neighborhood on the map or import a georeferenced orthomosaic. Keep its source, extent and coordinate information with the survey.

02 / REVIEW

Refine each building

Run building detection, inspect individual outlines and correct vertices. Review overlapping features and keep buildings distinct from parcels.

03 / DOCUMENT

Save usable records

Retain version history and measured area/perimeter. Export GeoJSON, CSV and survey PDFs for review and further GIS work.

Public demonstration

Explore without an account

When the live session is running, each visitor gets a temporary workspace. Use sample data, try the map and review workflow, and download your example results.

Physical drone connections are disabled in the public demo. Actual flight, camera capture and raw-photo stitching are not implemented. Local hardware compatibility still needs a field test.

The demo runs on the team's laptop. Its HTTPS address can change after a restart and stops working when the laptop or tunnel goes offline. Run your own local copy ↗
