DroneMap AI workspace guide

GeoVision · SIH26012 · The real Building segmentation model runs on this device. No account or password is needed in this browser edition.

Saved in this browser, on this device. Projects, imagery, records and history are not synchronized to a cloud account. Export records regularly and keep original images separately. Clearing site data, private browsing or browser storage eviction can remove the workspace.

Map, detect, review, export

  1. Create or select a project. Navigate to a neighborhood and draw a small scan rectangle.
  2. Run detection. The first scan downloads the actual model and WebAssembly runtime; speed depends on your device.
  3. Inspect each proposed roof. Edit vertices, remove false positives and draw missing boundaries. Add notes and save the records you reviewed.
  4. In Registry, inspect saved versions, check overlaps and export GeoJSON or CSV. Open a saved feature to download its Survey PDF.
  5. Reload and reopen the workspace to confirm saved records remain. Keep exports before changing browsers or devices.

Building outlines do not establish ownership parcels. Confidence is a model score, not independently measured accuracy. A reviewed record records your action in this workspace; it is not approval by an authority.

Import a source image

Use Sources to import a georeferenced unsigned 8-bit RGB GeoTIFF in EPSG:4326, EPSG:3857 or WGS84 UTM. A PNG/JPEG needs known WGS84 west, south, east and north bounds. Inspect the metadata and preview before scanning.

  • Imports: up to 32 MiB, 16 million pixels and 16 native inference windows. Crop larger imagery with GIS software first.
  • Map acquisition: at most 500 m per side and a 2,048-pixel export edge. Select a smaller area when requested.
  • Detection uses native image detail; display previews may be resampled. Unsupported inputs produce an error.

A GeoTIFF is not proof of drone capture or ground accuracy. Raw overlapping drone photos need an external orthomosaic/orthorectification workflow first. This application does not stitch raw photos or reliably place an unreferenced screenshot.

Drone survey planning

Draw a rectangular or convex area, open Drone, and enter the intended altitude, speed, overlap and actual camera specifications. Preview a coverage route and download the QGroundControl .plan for ground-station review.

This browser edition does not connect to aircraft telemetry, arm, fly, upload missions or trigger a camera. The route is a preview using a PX4 multirotor template. Set the actual vehicle, firmware and home elevation, configure camera triggering and complete the takeoff/return/landing workflow in a compatible ground station. Estimates assume level terrain and exclude obstacles, wind, turns and battery reserve. Hardware capture still needs field testing.

Keep useful copies

GeoJSON/CSV preserve feature records; keep PDFs and original imagery separately. These exports do not restore the complete browser database, images or edit history. Reimported GeoJSON opens reference drafts for review. Full browser-workspace backup/restore and cross-device sync are not implemented.

Survey PDFs are unsigned local records with available source/version information. They have no public verification QR and are not title deeds or government certificates.

Before presenting at SIH

Use permitted sample imagery. Load the app and finish a real scan on the presenting computer before judges arrive. Show a corrected outline, a saved record, a PDF/GIS export, successful reload and the route preview. Keep exported files and a recorded walkthrough for fallback. Internet is needed to load the app/model and fetch online imagery/search; browser caching is not a guaranteed offline installation.

Be clear about the next validation steps: independent local reference labels, authoritative parcel evidence and a real drone field test. A passing software test or high confidence value does not establish mapping accuracy.
